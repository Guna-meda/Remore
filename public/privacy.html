Privacy Policy

Last updated:

1. Overview

Remore ("Remore," "we," "us") provides a personal reminder and memory assistant that you interact with over WhatsApp. This policy explains what information we collect when you use Remore, how we use it, who we share it with, and the choices you have. By using Remore, you agree to the practices described here.

2. Information We Collect

  • Your WhatsApp phone number, collected when you sign up so we can message you on WhatsApp and identify your account.
  • Approximate country/location, detected from your IP address at signup (via a third-party geolocation lookup) solely to pre-select the correct country code for your phone number.
  • Messages you send Remore — text, images, and voice notes — and the content Remore extracts from them (e.g. text read from a photo, a transcription of a voice note) in order to create reminders, save notes, and respond to you.
  • Reminders, notes, and preferences you ask Remore to remember, stored so Remore can act on them and recall them later in conversation.
  • Google Calendar data, only if you choose to connect a Google account: your calendar's email address, the OAuth access needed to read and manage events on your behalf, and the event details (titles, times, attendees) Remore reads or creates as part of fulfilling your requests. This is only collected after you explicitly complete Google's own consent screen — nothing is accessed beforehand.
  • Basic technical/usage data generated by operating the service (e.g. message timestamps, delivery logs) needed to run and troubleshoot Remore.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To operate the core service: creating, updating, and delivering reminders; saving and recalling notes and preferences; responding to what you send.
  • To read from and write to your Google Calendar, only when you've connected an account and only to carry out the specific request you made.
  • To improve the reliability and accuracy of Remore's replies.
  • To communicate with you about your account or the service (e.g. confirming a calendar connection succeeded).

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use your data to serve you advertising.

4. Third Parties We Work With

Remore relies on the following service providers to operate. Each processes data on our behalf, under their own security and privacy terms:

  • Twilio — delivers and receives your WhatsApp messages.
  • OpenAI — processes your messages, images, and voice notes to understand requests and generate replies.
  • Google — provides Calendar access, only if and when you connect an account.
  • MongoDB Atlas — stores your account data, reminders, and notes.
  • Render — hosts the Remore application.
  • A third-party IP geolocation lookup, used once at signup only to guess your country for the phone number field.

5. Google User Data

Remore's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Calendar data accessed through Google is used solely to provide the reminder and scheduling features you request, is never used for advertising, and is never sold. You can disconnect a Google account from Remore at any time by asking Remore to disconnect it, or by revoking access directly from your Google Account permissions page.

6. Data Retention & Deletion

We keep your reminders, notes, and account information for as long as your account is active, so Remore can continue to work as intended. You can ask Remore to delete a specific reminder or note at any time. To delete your entire account and all associated data, or to disconnect a connected Google account, contact us using the details below.

7. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information, including encrypting sensitive credentials (such as Google account tokens) at rest. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Children's Privacy

Remore is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above.
